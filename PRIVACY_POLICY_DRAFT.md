## What we collect

- **Your answers:** business type, state, time in business, revenue range, loan purpose, amount needed, credit score range, and monthly debt payments.

## How we use it

- To show which lender products you're likely to fit and why.
- To send your answers and contact details to a lender **you choose**, so that lender can contact you about financing.
- To keep a record of when you gave consent and which lender received your information.

## Who we share it with

Only the lender you pick, and only after you check the consent box for that lender. That lender first sees a summary without your name or contact details.
